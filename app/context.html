Saga of Context

This page summarizes the academic foundation of the Adaptive Coherence Protocol and clearly separates validated mechanisms from hypotheses.

Academic Core

  • Autonomic dysregulation → low HRV (RMSSD) and stress reactivity.
  • Closed loop: measure (rPPG) → decide (micro-protocol) → actuate → re-measure.
  • Compliance: no PHI in logs; pseudonymization via KMS-salted hash.

Emergent Coherence (Hypothesis Space)

We explore group coherence and intention-mediated effects as testable hypotheses. The app can run blinded sessions and analyze HRV deltas. Product language remains academic and non-medical.
