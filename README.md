# 🧠 101Monkeys — The Coherence Network

**A Federated Platform for Measuring and Training Non-Local Autonomic Coherence**

## 📖 Overview: From Pacer to Network

The **101Monkeys Pacer Protocol** (Phase 1) established a robust, privacy-first, on-device sensor for individual autonomic regulation (ME/CFS, Dysautonomia).

**Phase 2, "The Coherence Network,"** expands this. We are evolving from a *personal pacer* into a **networked instrument** designed to investigate and quantify the very nature of autonomic coherence and non-local information.

Our core user base ("shut-ins") is uniquely situated: they are often in low-noise environments and are highly attuned to their internal autonomic states. This creates a perfect, high-sensitivity "sensor array" for this research.

1.  **The Stargate (SG) Hypothesis:** Drawing from declassified research on weak signal detection (e.g., Project Stargate), we hypothesize that a "successful" psi event is not a conscious thought, but a **biophysical signal**: an involuntary, precognitive **"autonomic flinch"** in the nervous system, which is typically lost in cognitive noise.

3.  **The 101Monkeys Synthesis:** Our technology is positioned to test this. We are **not** measuring "thoughts" or "energy." We are measuring their **direct, quantifiable, physiological correlates** using commodity phone sensors:
    *   **Vagal/Heart Coherence:** `RMSSD` (from PPG)
    *   **Sympathetic/Brain Coherence:** `PupilPV` (from the front camera, a proxy for Locus Coeruleus activity)

The **101Monkeys** app is, in effect, a **networked, high-sensitivity "biometric antenna."** The `(F_t, Y_actual)` pair is our core data point for both individual pacing and network-level signal detection.
